===== how to read this, and what it does not prove =====
Both NFR thresholds passed with a wide margin, p95 of 4.41 ms warm against a 300 ms target
and 66.13 ms cold against a 3 s target, with 0 failures across 6012 requests.

The margin is larger than it will be in a real measurement, and the reason matters.
No external provider was reachable in this run. Rental, fuel, and hotel all resolved to
their local fallbacks, and OSRM is a local container serving a clipped metro extract. So
the cold path here measures the fan-out, the cache, and the strategy engine, and it does
not measure network latency to a live provider, which is the part most likely to move p95.
Treat this as a floor on performance and a working harness, not as the NFR1 and NFR2
baseline. The real baseline runs are scheduled for the week ending 18 October, which is
risk R9 in docs/RISK_LOG.md.

It does close one thing. SC5 is judged at 2.5 s while the application deadline is 3 s,
logged as I-04. This run cannot distinguish them, which is the point of the issue.
